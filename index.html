Task types
One-off - A single task with one due date. Done once, then complete.
Floating - No fixed date. Set an urgency level (this week, this month, no rush).
Recurring - fixed schedule - Repeats on the same day or date regardless of when you complete it. Good for things like trash day (every Tuesday) or rent (1st of every month).
Recurring - from completion - Resets X days from whenever you actually finish it. Good for things like changing the AC filter (every 30 days from last service).
Tip: swipe right to complete, swipe left to snooze. Long-press to multi-select.
